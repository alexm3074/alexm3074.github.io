## ✨ What CCBaga Does for You

CCBaga is organized around four simple ideas. Each one has its own place in the app, so you never have to hunt for anything.

**🎭 Claude identities** — Keep your different Claude identities separate and organized. Instead of juggling browser profiles and notes, you see them in one list, clearly labeled. Switching between them is a click, not a chore.

**🕵️ Browser fingerprints** — Websites can quietly collect details about your browser. CCBaga shows you what those details look like and helps you keep them consistent, which is useful when you want a clean, predictable browsing environment.

**⚙️ System settings** — A tidy overview of the settings that actually matter for a clean workspace. See what is on, what is off, and change things without digging through menus you have never seen before.

**🌐 Network Skill** — A built-in check that tells you whether your network environment behaves the way it should. No technical reading required: the app runs the check and shows you a plain result.

Everything is local. CCBaga works on your computer and does not require you to create an account or sign in anywhere.
